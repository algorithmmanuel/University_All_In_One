# EduCyber owner beta playthrough

The owner will play the bundled release as an invited student, with further feature development paused. Use beta (`http://127.0.0.1:3000/journey` or the existing HTTPS tunnel), not development port 3010. Existing accounts, enrolments, scores, feedback and saved games should remain available.

| Stage | Cover | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Discover | Six public preview questions; Scholarship deadline, Surprise approval and Shared computer workshops | Is the first action clear? Are explanations accessible to a beginner? |
| 2 Understand | Three foundation courses (Python optional); applied modules; Invoice, Changing role and Backup dependency workshops | Does teaching prepare you for the decisions? Any unexplained terms? |
| 3 Check | Quick and longer course assessments; all ten practice topics; mistake review; three diagnostic workshops | Correct explanations, believable recommendations, saved results and suitable question lengths |
| 4 Investigate | All ten Cyber Explorers, ten Cyber Academy and ten Cyber Command missions; try both 2D/3D views; three investigation workshops | Evidence interaction, hints, keyboard/mobile controls, decisions and post-mission review |
| 5 Manage consequences | Defend the branch, Supplier Access Crisis, Recovery Under Pressure; three control/tradeoff workshops | Meaningful consequences, resource clarity, saved reopening and business tradeoffs |
| 6 Command | Branch Siege, same-incident replay/comparison; Clinical containment, Reconnection and Executive change workshops | Dependencies, uncertain signals, recovery sequence and decision explanations |
| 7 Face an opponent | Six Red/Blue scenarios against the computer; both seats and selected difficulty levels; three adversarial-reasoning workshops | Sealed choices, dated intelligence, useful evidence, predictable controls and understandable debriefs |
| 8 Compete and reflect | Solo role reversal and finished-match debriefs; three multiplayer/review workshops; optionally a two-human match with another eligible tester | What changes when you exchange perspectives? Does the review explain consequences and limitations? |

Workshop library: **24 cases, three decisions each**. Complete each once; replay at least one from each stage with another approach. Existing full-game catalogue remains 30 guided missions + four defender scenarios + six Red/Blue scenarios. These counts describe different activity types, not interchangeable game engines. Practice has 500 questions; use manageable topic sessions rather than treating all 500 as an entry barrier.

Start by verifying an old course, assessment, mission and saved game. Check branding and dark-red contrast on desktop and phone, return links, browser Back/reload, logout and account independence. Resume mid-activity. Report any lost or unexpected progress immediately; avoid repeatedly starting duplicate attempts after a connection error. Retry the same pending decision or refresh saved state.
