# Red/Blue V3 — Advanced Microfinance pilot (approved owner direction 5 November 2026)

## Status and release boundary
This is the **new V3 pilot specification**, not a claim that V3 gameplay is implemented. The owner approves a single higher-fidelity **Microfinance** scenario first, with the other five existing scenarios and all previous match/replay editions left intact until pilot acceptance. The existing V2 90-second/30-round mechanics remain operational and unchanged. Build the full V3 pilot in coordinated Development bundles, run automated gates and a two-account owner playthrough, then request explicit Beta promotion. No Beta data, auth, mail, invitations, lessons or ngrok changes. Do not introduce working exploit payloads, real external targets, or a generative AI component.

## Core product contract
- **Prepare:** Blue owns the full, actionable asset inventory and configures a bounded network, policy baseline, tool deployment, segmentation and patch priorities with finite credits. Red cannot see Blue configuration. Red selects limited recon approaches/capabilities without learning private network state. Policies express intent and may require technical controls, verification and enforcement; merely declaring a policy does not remove a vulnerability.
- **Engage:** One actor moves at a time (Blue opens under the established turn contract). Red starts with only exposed or otherwise legitimately discovered entry points and explicit uncertainty about undiscovered assets; reconnaissance progressively reveals nodes and directed trust links, assesses vulnerabilities with timestamps/confidence, and creates sensor-dependent Blue telemetry. Blue monitoring coverage, controls and privilege determine which events are visible and how certain they are. Post-action state resolves immediately; each seat sees only its own allowed projection. No action appears enabled solely because hidden truth would make it feasible. Red may act on outdated beliefs.
- **Inspect:** Every known asset is clickable and opens an accessible detail panel/dialog, mobile-compatible, with Blue-owned configuration, patch posture, policies, service dependencies, alerts/logs and remediation actions; the Red panel shows only discovered properties, qualified findings, age/confidence and available recon/attack options. Asset count, undiscovered indicators, last-observed version, and severity are explanatory, not an unauthorised full inventory leak. Do not reveal the exact number, IDs, existence, value or layout of unknown assets to Red; say more assets **may** exist.
- **Conclude:** Deterministic impact and objectives tied to financial-services availability, confidentiality, integrity, fraud, service cost, staff/customer harm and business value. End-of-match transcript and measured, qualified alternatives available only to authenticated participants after terminal state. Keep Learning vs Realistic assistance separate from engine truth and probability.

## Replace fixed round-count victory with two server-authoritative deadlines
1. **Match duration** is selected from 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes; default 30. The immutable match deadline is established on server at Engage start after both seats accept settings; preparatory configuration has its own bounded ready flow, and never silently consumes engagement time. Both humans must accept the same duration/turn limit before the clock starts. In bot mode the human chooses from authorised presets. Configuration cannot be changed mid-match.
2. **Maximum thinking time per action** is 30, 60, 90, 180 or 300 seconds; default 90. A separate turn deadline is assigned on every change of active seat. When the actor acts early, the next actor starts immediately. The **effective** deadline is min(turn_start + chosen_limit, immutable_match_deadline). Both clocks are represented on the UI and derived from server_now; browser time never decides expiry.
3. **No fixed number of gameplay rounds or turns.** Actions can continue as often as players decide before match end, subject to one legal action per turn, budget, prerequisites, abuse/rate limits and finite server capacity. No artificial 12- or 30-round victory trigger. Do not use V2 `MAX_ROUNDS`, V2 replay move count or finite V2 seed/replay constraints as V3 state limits. Preserve a bounded, paginated event log and compact/checkpointed replay verified by deterministic hashes to avoid unbounded payload size. Technical resource controls must not silently end a match as a gameplay defeat.
4. **Timeout policy:** a human who does not commit before their turn deadline automatically takes a server-persisted pass, then the other seat gets a fresh turn. Passing costs no action credits but does not freeze the **overall wall clock**. Record explicit pass/timeout events; a player cannot deliberately extend the match by cycling turns or refreshing. For the pilot, do **not** enable timeout-forfeit or unbounded pauses. Connection-grace design must be adapted from V2 but **never extend the absolute match deadline**. Exactly once policy, if retained, must be documented, tested and visible, with grace bounded by remaining match time.
5. **Overall expiry is terminal, even mid-turn.** The server atomically settles the match at its absolute deadline; no new action is accepted at/after expiry, including a previously opened turn and a duplicate attempt that would change committed history. In-flight requests are ordered under a database row lock with the authoritative timestamp. A worker must resolve expiry independent of page requests. Delayed worker restart must use the persisted deadline, not reset it.
6. **Win/draw:** An independently defined Red objective can end play early; Blue can end early only if a real, specified defensive victory condition is achieved. At time expiry score **both** sides based on outcome and business impact. Blue wins for successfully preserved business objectives if Red did not achieve its required objective; Red may win on a thresholded cumulative outcome if the scenario explicitly provides it. Ties/draws only if the documented, deterministic scoring contract says the outcomes are genuinely tied. Do not automatically draw due to a missed move, inactivity, or time expiration. Establish the scoring formula before engine code and test sample traces; do not invent it during implementation.
7. **Fairness:** Both seats see identical accepted match and turn durations. No player can set a different deadline, stop the clock via frontend pause, exploit stale submissions, reuse command IDs with a changed action, or submit after a server-persisted expiry. In a computer match, actions are drawn solely from the bot's role-filtered observation and resolved independently of a browser tab. Include turn replay, timeout/pass races, disconnects, server restart, and 2 separate authenticated sessions.

## Initial player experience
Blue opens a Preparation dashboard (network zones, asset inventory, dependencies, policy library, monitoring and patch status, deployment costs); Red sees only public organisational context and a reconnaissance strategy. Upon Engagement Red must be able to see **useful** reconnaissance options for currently known surfaces, plus explanations of why later attacks are locked; selected-asset dropdown alone must never hide permitted alternative targets. A successful recon gradually exposes a richer network, while a monitored probe may produce a meaningful alert in Blue's specific sensor panel. Asset click opens a detailed, permission-scoped inspector with remediation/evidence history and role-specific actions.

## Stage grouping, requested large bundle and pilot-first strategy
- **V3-A (authoritative domain):** versioned Microfinance graph, private truth vs per-role intelligence, inventory + policy/control/effect models, discovery and visibility transitions, immutable event provenance and safety tests. One secure vertical slice, not scattered cosmetic commits.
- **V3-B (time and engine):** mutable gameplay with unlimited turns during a bounded wall-clock match, immutable overall and per-turn clocks, wait auto-pass, durable worker, deterministic event checkpointing and terminal scoring. This must be implemented with explicit compatibility with V1/V2 recordings.
- **V3-C (full UI):** Blue preparation, role-scoped asset dialog, discovery topology, actionable alerts, available action browser, both clock displays, Learning/Realistic assistance and post-match analysis.
- **V3-D (one release):** all six current scenarios unaffected; dedicated deterministic tests plus isolated PostgreSQL transaction/worker, real API two-account, Playwright desktop/mobile, accessibility, secrecy and replay/balance tests. Realistic red discovery -> monitored Blue alert -> Blue intervention -> Red stale intelligence is the principal acceptance journey. Owner reviews one entire Microfinance pilot prior to adapting remaining five scenarios.

## Time/realism acceptance examples
- Start 30-minute/90-second match at server second 1000 -> game ends at 2800. Blue acts at 1040 -> Red's deadline is 1130; all deadlines still capped at 2800.
- Player acts at 2790 with a 90-second limit -> the next turn has at most 10 seconds, not 90.
- Player does not act by own deadline -> one persisted auto-pass; opponent gains the next turn, original overall deadline unchanged.
- Both players act quickly and may execute far more than 12 actions/rounds before the match deadline, subject to resource limits; gameplay does not terminate simply because move 12 or 30 was reached.
- Blue's monitoring can surface probes based on coverage even when no compromise occurred. Red never receives Blue's confidential logs or a count of undiscovered database assets.
- Red discovers an application server -> sees a possible database trust relationship, checks a suspected weakness -> Blue may detect the probe, apply segmentation or patch, and Red's previous assessment becomes stale rather than clairvoyantly updated.
- Full state/terminal outcome/replay remains identical after worker restart, concurrent command/expiry race and reconnect. No unjustified draw at time expiry.

## Explicit non-goals / questions to finalise within development
The owner now explicitly authorises a **bounded preparation/configuration pilot** for Microfinance, superseding the V2 design-only deferral for that one scenario. Do not extend construction to other scenarios or allow arbitrary uploaded network definitions yet. Preserve a finite set of policy/control/asset placements and the existing educational, non-operational nature of offensive actions. Score weights and preparatory ready/timeout defaults must be documented and tested before integration; the main game-duration and turn-duration choices above are the approved design baseline.
